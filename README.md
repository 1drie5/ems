## I mistook motion for progress

> **August 2026**
>
> I am here, unemployed, trying to learn something,
> staring at errors I don't understand,
> and writing code I don't really understand.
>
> I tell myself I am learning along the way.
> Maybe I am.
> Or maybe I am just typing along with a tutorial,
> changing things until they work,
> and calling that progress.
>
> I graduated last month.
> Four years of potential, and what do I have to show for them?
>
> I watched myself waste time.
> I procrastinated.
> I kept moving without really going anywhere.
>
> I hate the way I code.
> I hate how little I understand.
> I hate knowing I could have been much further
> if I had simply stopped wasting my own time.
>
> There is something particularly painful
> about knowing you had potential
> and watching it rot while you do nothing with it.
>
> I am suffocating in my own stagnation,
> and somehow I am still the one keeping myself there.
>
> Maybe someone finds this years from now.
> Maybe I finally become the person I keep imagining.
>
> If that happens, I don't want to romanticize this version of myself.
> I hated being this person.
> I hated the wasted years.
> I hated the excuses.
> I hated watching myself fall behind.
>
> But this is where I am.
>
> Perhaps I will read this again someday.
> I wonder who will be reading it then.
> For now, I leave it here.
>
> — [@1drie5](https://github.com/1drie5)
